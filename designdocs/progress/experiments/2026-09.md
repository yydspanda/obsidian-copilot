# September 2026 Experiments

## Evidence limitations and prerequisites

The two historical runs below recorded the upstream commit and test-file hash, but not a
checkoutable fork-source snapshot, archived canonical config/data bytes, or Node/npm/Jest versions.
The original hashes and observations are retained as evidence, not a claim of exact reproducibility.
DeepSeek did not expose an immutable model revision; the requested and observed model names are
recorded without inventing one.

The live suite requires `DEEPSEEK_API_KEY` in the environment or ignored `.env.test`; without it,
the live scenarios are skipped rather than passed. Node must support the host-process APIs used by
the test (`getBuiltinModule` and `loadEnvFile`). Do not record credentials in this log.
